# ART

A home for your stories.

ART is a Windows reader with a paper and ink interface, a personal library, source extensions, and offline companions.

## Install

Download **ART-Setup.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/omwfh/ART/releases/latest). Open it, choose a folder and shortcuts, select your starting sources, and let setup prepare ART. An internet connection is required during installation.

Open ART from its Start menu or Desktop shortcut. The launcher checks for updates before opening your reading room. You can also check in **Settings → About → App updates**.

Updating preserves your library, settings, sources, and reading progress. Uninstalling preserves your profile and external book folders.

## Downloads

This repository distributes compiled Windows x64 releases and update files. The development source is maintained separately. You do not need a GitHub account to install ART or receive public updates.

Third-party notices are available in ART and ART Setup.
